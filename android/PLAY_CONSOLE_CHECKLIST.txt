  see android/UPLOAD_KEY_FINGERPRINTS.txt

## Play Console
1. Sign in with the NEW Google account (complete $25 Play developer registration if needed).
2. Create app: Komodo, free/paid as appropriate.
3. Upload AAB to Internal testing first.
4. Store listing: title, short/full description, 512x512 icon (assets/icon/app_icon.png),
   at least 2 phone screenshots,
   1024x500 feature graphic: assets/store/play_feature_graphic.png
   (copy also at android/play_feature_graphic.png).
5. Privacy policy URL (required — login + FCM tokens).
6. Data safety: account info, device IDs, push tokens; no ads.
7. Content rating questionnaire (school app — declare age honestly).
8. Declare notifications / POST_NOTIFICATIONS in App content.
9. After first upload: Play Console → App signing → copy App signing key SHA-1
   → Firebase gecko-komodo Android app → add fingerprint.
